ACE Medical and KAT Advanced Medical body-image artwork
======================================================

PNG layers in `body/` were converted from the upstream `.paa` textures:

- ACE3 `addons/medical_gui/data/body_image/*.paa`
  https://github.com/acemod/ACE3
  GNU General Public License v2.0

- KAT Medical (KAM) `addons/gui/data/body_image/*.paa`
  https://github.com/Tomcat-SG/KAM
  GNU General Public License v3.0

These are the same stacked pictures ACE/KAT draw in the medical menu
(background silhouette, per-part fills, tourniquet/bone overlays, and KAT
treatment icons). Colouring is applied at runtime the way the games do:
`colorText` on each layer.
